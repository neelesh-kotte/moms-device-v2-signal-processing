03 / SLEEP AUDIO

SleepSignal

Map low-energy intervals and changes in overnight audio. Quiet intervals are candidates for review—not evidence of apnea or a breathing pause.

04 / HEART SOUNDS

MurmurMap

Surface sustained frequency-band regions in heart-sound clips. A spectral pattern is not a murmur label and cannot establish heart disease.

Responsible by design

No confident guesses disguised as care.

An acoustic pattern can be affected by the microphone, room, placement, movement, and person. A model can also learn shortcuts in a dataset. We expose those limits instead of hiding them.

Not a diagnostic device

Nothing on this site diagnoses, rules out, or treats a health condition. It is for research and software development.

Candidate means candidate

Segment flags come from simple thresholds. They are not expert-verified wheezes, crackles, apnea events, murmurs, or bowel events.

Keep sensitive audio private

Analysis in this tool runs locally, but public-data and future model workflows still require careful data governance.

Use clinical care for health concerns

Do not use experimental audio analysis to make medical decisions. Ask a qualified clinician about symptoms or health concerns.
