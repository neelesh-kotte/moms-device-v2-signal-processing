01 / V1 → V2 → V3

Three versions, each built around a harder question.

The project moved from basic capture feasibility, to controlled acquisition and held-out evaluation, to a human-in-the-loop research prototype that makes signal quality and uncertainty visible.

V1 · Capture

Can the chain hear the signal?

Established a low-cost ESP32, MAX4466 microphone amplifier, and stethoscope-style acoustic coupling as a workable abdominal-acoustic capture path.

V2 · Validation

Can the chain be controlled?

Added controlled acquisition, sample-rate checks, clipping checks, duplicate rejection, source-separated evaluation, and held-out analysis.

V3 · SenseLoop

Can the limits be visible?

Added guided sessions, quality gates, separated profiles, optional check-ins, privacy controls, and uncertainty-aware summaries that can abstain when evidence is insufficient.

03 / Independent operators

Could someone else follow the recording workflow?

Two independent people completed the physical recording workflow without direct supervision. The purpose was narrow: test whether the written process was usable beyond its designer.

Supports

The written procedure was usable by more than the designer in this small sample, including the same stethoscope coupling, MAX4466, ESP32 acquisition path, and quality checks.

Does not support

A population reliability estimate, universal operator agreement, inter-rater validity, or clinical reproducibility.

04 / External review

Technical critique came from outside the build.

Eight or more professional engineering reviewers contributed critique across chemical, mechanical, software, and other technical perspectives. This is external design feedback, not certification, endorsement, or journal peer review.

Why it matters

External critique helps expose assumptions in mounting, robustness, electronics, acquisition workflow, usability, and software behavior that are easy for a single builder to miss.

Boundary

Reviewer participation does not convert MOM into a clinically validated or certified device.

05 / External corpus

The public-data result is reproducible, including the negative primary result.

The external validation package processed 6,424 complete windows from 19 anonymized subject groups. Sixteen subject groups contained both classes and were eligible for the primary paired analysis.

Frozen primary endpoint
0.0089

Equal-subject mean event-minus-non-event median spectral-ratio difference. 95% bootstrap interval: −0.0151 to 0.0329. Exact two-sided sign-flip p = 0.455. The primary median-shift result is inconclusive.

Exploratory rank probability
0.598

Equal-subject mean probability. 95% bootstrap interval: 0.540 to 0.655. Exact two-sided sign-flip p = 0.0032. This exploratory result does not replace the primary endpoint.
