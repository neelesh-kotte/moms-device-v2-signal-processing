01 / VERSION HISTORY

V1 → V2 → V3

Each version answers a different engineering question. V3 is the product prototype; the public-corpus analysis is kept as a separate research track.

V1 · CAPTURE FEASIBILITY

Can the chain hear anything useful?

Established the ESP32, MAX4466 microphone amplifier, and stethoscope-style acoustic coupling as a workable low-cost capture chain.

V2 · CONTROLLED VALIDATION

Can the process survive scrutiny?

Added sample-rate and clipping checks, duplicate rejection, source-separated evaluation, calibration, and held-out analysis around the acquisition path.

V3 · SENSELOOP PLATFORM

Can it remain careful in real use?

Added guided sessions, quality gates, separated profiles, optional self-reports, privacy-safe Guest Mode, uncertainty-aware summaries, and “Not enough information” abstention.

03 / INDEPENDENT OPERATOR TEST

Could someone else follow the instructions?

Two independent people reproduced the physical recording workflow without direct supervision.

What this supports

  • The written workflow was usable by more than the designer in this small sample.
  • The same stethoscope coupling, MAX4466, ESP32 acquisition path, and quality checks were used.
  • The operators were not asked to produce a desired result.

What it does not support

  • A population reliability estimate.
  • Universal operator agreement or clinical reproducibility.
  • Device validity across people, positions, rooms, or environments.

Operators are anonymized. Names, health information, and private recordings are not needed for this aggregate engineering claim.

05 / EXTERNAL TECHNICAL REVIEW

Critique, not endorsement.

Eight or more professional engineering reviewers contributed feedback through volunteering, internships, and FBLA-related technical environments.

Perspectives recorded

Chemical, mechanical, and software/technical engineering perspectives informed discussion of sensor mounting, acoustic coupling, gain and clipping, data handling, reproducibility, failure modes, and public wording.

Boundary

The public record reports critique and feedback. It does not imply certification, authorship, medical validation, peer review of a manuscript, or endorsement by any reviewer or employer.

06 / LIMITS

What this package refuses to claim.

MOM is an engineering prototype. The public evidence package is designed to show the work clearly without stretching it into a medical conclusion.

MOM does not claim diagnosis, disease screening, objective hunger measurement, nutritional prescription, mind reading, or automatic food purchasing. Guest Mode contains demonstration content; private profiles and live device data remain outside the public evidence page.

Read the underlying records in the evidence folder or start with the repository README.
