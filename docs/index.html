Listen to abdominal sounds. Build your own research history.

MOM SenseLoop is an experimental device that records abdominal sounds through a stethoscope-style sensor. After a recording, you can add your own hunger rating. Over time, MOM compares new recordings with your previous examples and only shows an experimental pattern summary when there is enough usable information.

See how MOM works

Research and personal exploration only. Not a medical device or diagnostic tool.

How MOM works

You do not need to know anything about signal processing to use it.

1

Power MOM

After one-time setup, plug the ESP32 into a power bank. The saved firmware starts automatically. No Arduino connection is needed during normal use.

2

Place the sensor

Hold the stethoscope-style sensor at the same selected abdominal position and keep it reasonably steady.

3

Record for one minute

MOM captures the sound and checks whether the recording is clear enough to use.

4

Add your rating

You rate how hungry you feel. Your own rating is the reference MOM learns from.

What it can do

Guided recording

Walk through readiness, placement, recording, and saving without needing hardware knowledge.

Recording-quality review

Checks movement, background noise, clipping, contact consistency, and completion.

Separate profiles

Each signed-in user's profiles, recordings, ratings, preferences, and research history stay separate.

Careful summaries

If the available information is weak or conflicting, MOM says “Not enough information” instead of forcing a result.

History

Review previous sessions, ratings, recording quality, and whether a session was usable.

Optional food ideas

Preference-based ideas are kept separate from the acoustic research model and are not nutrition advice.

Guest demo

Made-up demonstration data only. No real profile or live sensor data is used.

What MOM does not claim

MOM can use

Abdominal recordings, recording quality, your optional ratings, optional meal timing, and saved preferences.

MOM does not determine

A diagnosis, medical safety, nutritional needs, treatment, or an objective “true hunger” value.
