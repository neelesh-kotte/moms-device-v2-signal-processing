Low-cost gastroacoustic sensing, tested across datasets.

The MOM Device project combines accessible sensing hardware with transparent physiological-acoustic analysis. The research track tests a fixed bowel-sound spectral ratio in seven derivation recordings and a separate 19-subject corpus while keeping the failed primary endpoint distinct from an exploratory rank pattern.

Fixed spectral estimator

Audio is mean-centered, resampled to 8 kHz, divided into non-overlapping 500 ms windows, tapered with numpy.hanning(4000), and transformed with a real FFT.

The feature is power(120–480 Hz) / power(20–2,000 Hz), using inclusive frequency boundaries. It was not retuned for the external corpus.

Cross-corpus analysis methodology

The design separates descriptive acoustic windows from independent biological units and preserves the difference between the frozen primary endpoint and post-hoc analyses.

Analyze the derivation corpusSeven public 48 kHz recordings produced 10,922 complete windows. Because participant and sensor mappings are unresolved, inference remains recording-level and conditional.
Reconstruct the external corpus1,606 two-second, 44.1 kHz clips and 6,413 source annotations were organized into 19 anonymized subject records; duplicate boundary-spanning annotations were removed.
Apply one fixed estimatorBoth corpora use the same 8 kHz resampling, 500 ms windows, boundary guard, Hann taper, FFT, and 120–480/20–2,000 Hz ratio.
Respect independent unitsPooled windows are descriptive. The external primary analysis gives each eligible subject one median difference and equal weight.
Test the frozen primary endpointThe subject-level event-minus-non-event median difference receives a t-based interval and exact two-sided sign-flip test.
Label exploratory work explicitlyAfter heterogeneity was observed, within-subject Mann–Whitney probability of superiority was added post hoc. It does not replace the primary endpoint.
Stress-test the resultChecks include participant bootstrapping, leave-one-subject-out analysis, minimum class counts, alternative labels, Holm correction, power decomposition, and estimator verification.

Absolute-power transport failed

External target, outside-target, and total power factors were 1.46, 0.99, and 1.08. Every corresponding interval included zero, so the derivation power-amplification pattern did not transfer.

Why the endpoints differ

Medians test one point in each distribution. Probability of superiority summarizes all event–non-event pair orderings. Similar medians can coexist with distributed rank separation, but the post-hoc rank result remains hypothesis-generating.

Expanded study

Transport checks

The manuscripts report exact sign-flip tests, participant bootstrap intervals, leave-one-subject-out means, minimum class-count checks, alternative label definitions, and Holm correction.

Estimator validation

Numerical verification

The expanded analysis reports 28 deterministic tests, 991 end-to-end resampling checks with maximum discrepancy 1.79 × 10⁻¹¹, and 100 independent periodogram comparisons with maximum difference 2.22 × 10⁻¹⁶.

Publication record

Research manuscripts

The original work was published through the Journal of Emerging Investigators and extended into a cross-corpus transport paper and Columbia Junior Science Journal research brief.

Limitations and scope

Research result, not a medical device. This work does not establish diagnostic accuracy, gastrointestinal motility measurement, a biological mechanism, a clinical threshold, or hardware safety and effectiveness.

Metadata limitation

Corpus 1 lacks verified participant and sensor mappings. The corpora also differ in formats, sensors, protocols, gains, and annotation systems.

Interpretation limitation

The external primary median shift and absolute-power mechanism did not transfer. The rank result was selected after the primary outcome and must remain exploratory.

Prototype limitation

Consumer-grade microphones and mechanical coupling remain sensitive to placement, motion, pressure, gain, and room noise. Hardware validation is separate from the public-data result.
