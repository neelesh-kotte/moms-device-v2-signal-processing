    <article class="card s3"><div class="n">1</div><h3>Power MOM</h3><p>After one-time firmware setup, plug the ESP32 into a power bank. It starts automatically. Arduino is not needed during normal use.</p></article>
    <article class="card s3"><div class="n">2</div><h3>Place the sensor</h3><p>Hold the stethoscope-style sensor at the same selected abdominal position and keep it comfortably steady.</p></article>
    <article class="card s3"><div class="n">3</div><h3>Record for one minute</h3><p>MOM captures the sound and checks movement, background noise, clipping, contact consistency, and completion.</p></article>
    <article class="card s3"><div class="n">4</div><h3>Add your check-in</h3><p>You can rate how hungry you feel and optionally add meal timing. Your own response stays the reference.</p></article>
