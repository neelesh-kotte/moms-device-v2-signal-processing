# External reviewer documentation

## Recorded claim

The MOM project received critique from 8+ professional engineering reviewers.

The reviewers were reached through the project’s volunteering, internship, and FBLA-related technical environments. The documented perspectives included chemical, mechanical, and software/technical engineering.

## What reviewers contributed

The reviews were used as design critique around issues such as:

- sensor and mounting choices;
- acoustic coupling and contact consistency;
- gain, clipping, and signal headroom;
- data handling and software behavior;
- reproducibility and failure-mode interpretation;
- whether public wording overclaimed what the prototype could show.

The contribution is documented as critique and feedback. It is not presented as endorsement, certification, authorship, medical validation, or peer review of a manuscript.

## Privacy boundary

Reviewer names, employer information, private correspondence, and personal contact details are not required to support the aggregate claim and are not published in this repository.

The public record therefore reports:

| Field | Public value |
|---|---|
| Reviewer count | 8+ |
| Broad perspectives | Chemical, mechanical, software/technical |
| Role | External engineering critique |
| Use in project | Design and wording feedback |
| Endorsement implied | No |

A stronger audit trail would attach an anonymized review index with reviewer code, date, artifact version, technical domain, issue raised, and resulting project change. This page does not invent those fields where they are not part of the public summary.
